# Dead Fire — balance pass (Phase 6, 2026-10-30)

How the race clock was tuned, with the measurements behind it. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-phase6-ship-design.md` §3.

## What was wrong

Each gate adds `CheckpointTimeBonus` to the clock, and a lap passes every gate. With the original 8 s per gate, a lap at the rivals' pace (about 32 s on PROVING GROUND, 4 gates) earned 32 s back: the clock barely moved, and a driver who kept pace never ran out of time (the runs below compute 23810 s on PROVING GROUND and 1132 s on SWITCHBACK). The race is meant to end by time.

## How it was measured

`tools/balance_sim.py` runs a race in the open editor's **Simulate** mode, where the GameMode spawns and drives the rivals with the real AI, items and clock, and samples every rival every 5 s. A rival's lap time is the track length over its mean speed along the track (from `DistanceAlongTrack`, unwrapped at the start line), so the 5 s sampling adds no error; the field's lap time **P** is the median. Simulate has no player and only a player's gates add time, so every simulated race ends at its clock **C**; the race a driver at the rivals' pace would get is computed:

    laps = C / (P - G x bonus)        race length = laps x P        (G = gates per lap)

and the bonus for a target number of laps N is `(N x P - C) / (N x G)`, rounded to 0.5 s and kept between 2 s and 8 s. The targets are 6 laps on PROVING GROUND and 4 on SWITCHBACK, about three minutes either way. The difficulty for a run comes from a save in the slot `DeadFireBalance` (the developer's `DeadFire.sav` is never read for it or written), and the tool restores everything it changes.

    python tools/balance_sim.py /Game/DeadFire/Maps/L_TestTrack --difficulty 1 --seconds 200 --target-laps 6
    python tools/balance_sim.py /Game/DeadFire/Maps/L_Switchback --difficulty 1 --seconds 230 --target-laps 4 --out docs/superpowers/balance-2026-10-30.md

## What was chosen

| Rule | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Checkpoint bonus, PROVING GROUND | 8 s | **3 s** | P 32.2 s (first run) gives 3.0 s for 6 laps; the second run (P 34.9 s) computes a 183 s race with it |
| Checkpoint bonus, SWITCHBACK | 8 s | **2 s** | P 46.1 s gives 1.72 s, kept at the 2 s floor; the second run (P 55.8 s) computes a 183 s race with it |
| Pass score | 300 / 400 | unchanged | 6 x 100 and 4 x 100 laps at pace are not below them; kills are the margin |
| NPC item interval (NORMAL) | 20 s | unchanged | 0 and 0.63 wrecks per rival per race, under the ceiling of 1 |
| Clock | 120 s / 150 s | unchanged | |
| Functional-test GameMode bonus | 8 s | 8 s (pinned) | the Phase 2 tests assert +8 s per gate |

A faster driver gains a little time per lap and races a little longer; a driver who stops for crates runs out sooner. Lap times vary noticeably from run to run (SWITCHBACK's median moved from 46 s to 56 s between two runs, one rival losing time in traffic), so treat one run as a sample and re-run before changing a number.

HARD and EASY were measured but not retuned. In Simulate the rivals have no player to attack, so all their items land on each other: on HARD five rivals scored six kills among themselves and the field's lap time (48.5 s) shows carnage, not pace. With a player in the race, rivals prefer the player as a target. The difficulty's speed scale (0.85 / 1.1) did not show within one run's noise; HARD is mostly felt through the two extra rivals and their item rate. The playtest checklist asks a person to judge both.
