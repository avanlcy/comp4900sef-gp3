# Dead Fire Phase 7 (Own Look, Own Feel) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Graph-authoring steps go through the generator (`tools/bpgen/graphs/*.py`), never by hand in the editor.

**Goal:** Give Dead Fire its own handling, make its existing items and shooting visible, audible and felt, and replace every stock asset a player sees or hears (cars, gear, weapons, items, props, road, gates, terrain, textures, materials, effects, sounds, HUD art) with generated Dead Fire assets, with every functional test green and the packaged build at 60 fps.

**Architecture:**
- Art is generated by scripts. `tools/artgen` (numpy/Pillow) makes textures, images, sounds and terrain; Blender 5.2 LTS headless makes meshes. Outputs go to the git-ignored `art/`, are imported by editor scripts into `/Game/DeadFire/Art`, and are checked by `df_verify_art.py`.
- Gameplay changes stay in the Blueprint generator: two new modules, `fx` and `environment`, plus changes to `vehicle`, `weapon`, `character`, `items`, `game_mode`, `checkpoint`, `track` and `hud`.
- The authoring plugin gains three functions: `SetComponentSocket`, `CreateLandscapeFromRaw` and `SetFontFace`.
- The level builder places the landscape, the scatter and the lighting.
- The live Blender MCP is the review loop for every mesh (spec §4.7), and it searches Poly Haven for surfaces or props that fail review (spec §4.6). Builds stay headless and scripted.

**Tech Stack:** UE 4.27.2 (`D:\UE_4.27`), Blueprint-only project, Python 3.11 + numpy 1.24 + Pillow 9.3, Blender 5.2.3 LTS (`C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2`), Blender MCP ("MCP for Blender" add-on 1.8 on 127.0.0.1:9876, Claude Code server `uvx mcp-for-blender`), Poly Haven public API, DF Blueprint Authoring plugin (C++, VS 2019 Build Tools), UE Functional Testing, MaterialEditingLibrary, AssetImportTask.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-phase7-art-feel-design.md`, cited below as "spec §n". Earlier designs: `2026-10-20-dead-fire-design.md` and the Phase 3–6 specs.

## Review Focus

These are the five failure modes the spec implies but no feature test would hit by accident. Each has a test in the owning task.

1. **SWITCHBACK's S-bend at 120 km/h on a keyboard.** Speed-sensitive steering must still let the car turn. `FT_DFDrive_TurnsAtSpeed` (Task 9): at least 45° of heading change in 1.5 s at full lock from 120 km/h, and the car is still upright.
2. **Seven cars drifting or burning at once** should not flood the world with smoke actors. Cosmetic smoke spawns only within 80 m of the player's view. `FT_DFFx_NoTyreSmokeFarAway` (Task 16).
3. **A restart or race end while effects, debris and decals are live.** Nothing stale may stay behind to block or confuse. `FT_DFFx_RestartClearsEffects` (Task 16, extended to debris in Task 21).
4. **New sounds must follow the master volume setting.** Every imported `SW_DF_*` uses `/Engine/EngineSounds/Master`, and every non-UI sound uses `SA_DF_World`. `ART VERIFY` checks both (Task 6).
5. **Look-back or drift state stuck** after exiting the car, a respawn or a pause. `FT_DFDrive_StateClearsOnExitAndRespawn` (Task 10).

- `textures.RECIPES: dict[str, Callable[[int seed, int size], dict[str, np.ndarray]]]`, returning the maps `BC` (RGB), `N` (RGB), `ORM` (R occlusion, G roughness, B metallic).
