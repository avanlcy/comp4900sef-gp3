# Dead Fire — balance re-check (Phase 7, 2026-10-31)

Phase 7 changed the cars' handling (the arcade layer) and the ground (terrain, scatter, new road meshes), so spec
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-30-phase7-art-feel-design.md` §10 asks for the rivals' pace to be measured again with
`tools/balance_sim.py`. If the NPC lap time moved more than 5 % from `docs/superpowers/balance-2026-10-30.md`, the
`LEVELS` bonus is recomputed with the Phase 6 formula and recorded here. The tool, the route and the formula are those of
the Phase 6 doc:

    bonus = (N x P - C) / (N x G), rounded to 0.5 s, kept between 2 s and 8 s   (N = 6 laps PROVING GROUND, 4 SWITCHBACK)

## Result

Three runs per track at NORMAL; P is each run's median rival lap. The measured P is the median of the three runs. Phase 6
measured two NORMAL runs per track, so the change is given against their mean and against each.

| Track | Phase 6 P (runs) | Phase 7 P (runs) | Measured P | Change vs Phase 6 mean (vs each run) | Trigger (> 5 %) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PROVING GROUND | 32.2, 34.9 (mean 33.55) | 27.7, 32.1, 35.3 | **32.1 s** | **−4.3 %** (−0.3 %, −8.0 %) | no (yes against the second Phase 6 run alone) |
| SWITCHBACK | 46.1, 55.8 (mean 50.95) | 42.3, 41.1, 44.4 | **42.3 s** | **−17.0 %** (−8.2 %, −24.2 %) | **yes** |

| Rule | Before | Formula with the measured P | After | Race length for a driver at pace |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Checkpoint bonus, PROVING GROUND | 3 s | (6 × 32.1 − 120) / 24 = 3.03 → 3.0 | **3 s (no change)** | 192 s (6.0 laps) |
| Checkpoint bonus, SWITCHBACK | 2 s | (4 × 42.3 − 150) / 20 = 0.96 → 1.0 → floor 2.0 | **2 s (no change)** | 196 s (4.6 laps) |
| Functional-test GameMode bonus | 8 s (pinned) | – | 8 s | – |

SWITCHBACK's rivals are faster, but the formula already sat on its 2 s floor in Phase 6 (1.72 s from P 46.1); a faster
field only moves the raw value further below it, so the bonus stays 2 s. PROVING GROUND's measured P gives back the 3 s
Phase 6 chose. `tools/bpgen/df.py` `LEVELS` keeps its values; only its comment now cites this doc.

For this measured P, the choice of Phase 6 baseline run does not change the bonuses. **The choice of P does.** Each run's
own P gives a different PROVING GROUND bonus:

| Track | Run | Wrecks | P (s) | Raw (N × P − C) / (N × G) | Bonus at that P |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PROVING GROUND | 1 | 0 | 27.7 | 1.93 | 2.0 s |
| PROVING GROUND | 2 | 2 | 32.1 | 3.03 | 3.0 s |
| PROVING GROUND | 3 | 6 | 35.3 | 3.83 | 4.0 s |
| SWITCHBACK | 1 | 1 | 42.3 | 0.96 | 2.0 s (floor) |
| SWITCHBACK | 2 | 2 | 41.1 | 0.72 | 2.0 s (floor) |
| SWITCHBACK | 3 | 0 | 44.4 | 1.38 | 2.0 s (floor) |

**Method:** P is the median of three Simulate runs, with the rivals' wrecks and respawns included. Phase 6 measured its
baseline the same way: its NORMAL runs include 0–2 wrecks and 3–9 stuck/flip respawns per run. So like is compared with
like.

**Why not a wreck-free P:** it would measure a different thing from that baseline. PROVING GROUND's wreck-free run
(run 1, P 27.7 s) would fire the trigger and give 2 s. Its clean rival's 26.6–27.7 s would do the same. In Simulate the
rivals have only each other to shoot, so their fights are part of what the tool measures. (Controller ruling, Task 23
review: PROVING GROUND stays 3 s.) The spread between runs is the reason for taking three.

At 2 s SWITCHBACK runs a little past its 4-lap target (196 s against about 170 s at P 42.3 s); the 2 s floor is the
Phase 6 rule and was kept. HARD and EASY were not re-measured (Phase 6 did not retune them either).

## How it was measured

    python tools/balance_timestep.py -- python tools/balance_sim.py /Game/DeadFire/Maps/L_TestTrack --difficulty 1 --seconds 2700 --target-laps 6
    python tools/balance_timestep.py -- python tools/balance_sim.py /Game/DeadFire/Maps/L_Switchback --difficulty 1 --seconds 3000 --target-laps 4
    python tools/balance_timestep.py -- python tools/balance_sim.py /Game/DeadFire/Maps/L_Switchback --difficulty 1 --seconds 2700 --target-laps 4

- All three PROVING GROUND runs used the first command. SWITCHBACK run 1 used the second, runs 2 and 3 the third.
- Each run also passed `--out` (the evidence file) to `balance_sim.py`, and an editor-log path to `balance_timestep.py`
  (the respawn counts below come from it).
- `--seconds` is a wall-clock limit; every run ended on the race clock (C = 120 s / 150 s of game time).

**The frame rate matters, so the route gained one wrapper: `tools/balance_timestep.py`.** Its editor side is
`comp4900sef/Script/df_balance_timestep.py`.
- **Why:** physics substeps at 1/60 s, at most 6 per frame, so a frame advances physics by at most 0.1 s. Below about
  10 fps physics falls behind the game clock. The cars cover less ground per game second than their speed says, and P
  comes out too long.
  - In a 4 fps Simulate run on SWITCHBACK (1 s samples), the rivals' positions advanced at about 32 km/h while their
    speedometers averaged about 67 km/h.
  - Phase 6's runs simulated at 72–119 fps (the editor log's frame counter).
  - On 2026-10-31 the editor shared the machine with other CPU-heavy programs and dropped to 3–30 fps at times.
- **What the wrapper does:** it holds each frame's game step near 1/60 s for the length of the command, and is a no-op
  at 60 fps or more (see its docstring).
- **Provenance:** the runs here used the same code from a scratch copy, before it was added to the repo. The editor side
  is identical; the wrapper differs only in its paths.

| Run | Editor fps (mean) | Mean game step (s) | Frames over the 0.1 s physics budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| PROVING GROUND 1 / 2 / 3 | 72 / 100 / 65 | 0.0108 / 0.0100 / 0.0119 | 6 / 2 / 11 (the start-up frames) |
| SWITCHBACK 1 / 2 / 3 | 101 / 74 / 105 | 0.0099 / 0.0122 / 0.0095 | 2 / 3 / 3 |

## Why the earlier re-check (Task 22) showed a bigger move

Task 22's single runs gave PROVING GROUND 39.5 s (+13 %) and SWITCHBACK 39.3 s (−30 %) against the second Phase 6 runs.

1. **PROVING GROUND +13 % was the frame rate.** By the log's frame counter, Task 22's PROVING GROUND run simulated at
   3–8 fps for its first minute, below the 10 fps physics limit (then 24–113 fps). Its SWITCHBACK run stayed at 17–95 fps.
   At a valid frame rate PROVING GROUND is not slower than Phase 6.
2. **Most of SWITCHBACK's −30 % was the baseline run.** Phase 6's second SWITCHBACK run (55.8 s) had 9 stuck/flip
   respawns; its first (46.1 s) had 4, as did these runs (4, 3, 5). Against the first run the change is −8 %.
3. **The rest is a real gain in clean-lap pace from the Phase 7 car.** The AI is unchanged since Phase 6, so the gain
   comes from the car. The fastest rival of each run went from 31.6 and 32.9 s (Phase 6) to 26.6, 27.7 and 27.6 s on
   PROVING GROUND (means −15 %), and from 43.5 and 46.5 s to 37.7, 39.7 and 38.9 s on SWITCHBACK (means −14 %). One run with the arcade grip assist, downforce and
   stability damping set to 0 on the rivals' cars gave rivals of 28.3–35.6 s (P 30.6 s). That is between the two, so the
   assists are part of the gain. One run cannot separate them from the lower centre of mass (not switched off) or from noise.
4. **Not the cause:**
   - Friction: the old road (BlackGrid), the old ground (LandscapeNoHole), the new road (MI_DF_Road) and both terrains
     all use DefaultPhysicalMaterial (friction 0.7). The road meshes have no body physical material, and the Sedan
     wheels have no tyre config.
   - Route: the AI follows the main road's spline and has no shortcut logic. Sampled every second on SWITCHBACK, the
     rivals were never on the shortcut.
   - Stuck spots: free respawns are within Phase 6's range on both tracks.
5. **The remaining spread is fighting.** In Simulate the rivals have only each other to shoot. PROVING GROUND's three runs
   had 0, 2 and 6 wrecks, and the median follows them (27.7 / 32.1 / 35.3 s) while the clean rival's lap stays at
   26.6–27.7 s. Phase 6's NORMAL PROVING GROUND runs had none. With a player in the race, the rivals aim at the player.

Respawns per run (editor log; a respawn within 5 s of the same rival's death is a wreck respawn, the rest are free:
stuck, flipped or lost):

| | Phase 6 run 1 / 2 | Phase 7 run 1 / 2 / 3 |
|---|---|---|
| PROVING GROUND wrecks | 0 / 0 | 0 / 2 / 6 |
| PROVING GROUND free respawns | 3 / 4 | 3 / 3 / 3 |
| SWITCHBACK wrecks | 2 / 0 | 1 / 2 / 0 |
| SWITCHBACK free respawns | 4 / 9 | 4 / 3 / 5 |
